Tokimori is designed to use limited information. It has no dedicated user account or Tokimori server. This page describes Tokimori 1.1.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

The app stores settings in its own preferences and shares data needed for restrictions with its Screen Time extension in an App Group on your iPhone:

  • Allowed apps and websites: Apple-issued Family Controls identifiers (tokens). Tokimori does not read the app or website names from these identifiers; iOS renders their names and icons.
  • Time settings and sessions: the prepared duration or time of day, session identifiers, and start and end times for an active lock.
  • Legacy saved settings: data from earlier trial builds may remain locally; saved-setting slots are not exposed in this update.
  • Automatic-lock schedules: up to three weekday/start/end combinations, a saved time-zone identifier, an enabled state, allowed-app/website identifiers, and identifiers and timestamps used to manage schedule revisions and prevent duplicate starts.
  • Completed sessions: session identifiers and start/end times. The app does not record which apps or websites you used, their content, or time spent using them. History has no automatic expiry in Tokimori.
  • Free-session usage: a baseline timestamp and identifiers for up to five counted starts. The app and extension share this information so manual and automatic starts use the same allowance. Version 1.1 copies an existing allowance into that shared store when needed.
  • Purchase access: a locally cached expiry timestamp derived from verified subscription information, used to decide whether a scheduled start is permitted. A cached expiry can become stale until the app verifies the purchase state again.
  • Diagnostics and preferences: up to 30 diagnostic event entries per source (app and extension), containing event names and timestamps, plus notification and introduction preferences. Diagnostic events do not contain selected app names or browsing content.

Tokimori does not automatically upload these settings, selections, session records, or diagnostics to the operator. Apple and the operating system provide the Screen Time and purchase services described below.

2. Local notifications

End notifications are optional local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, including for automatic sessions when enabled. Their message is fixed text and contains no app or website information. Locks do not require notification permission. iOS can delay or hide notifications.

3. Purchases through Apple

Monthly and annual subscriptions use Apple’s App Store through StoreKit. Loading products, making/restoring purchases, and checking purchase state communicate with Apple. Tokimori checks transaction information such as product identifiers, verification, revocation, and expiry to handle purchases and access.

Tokimori does not receive your card number or Apple Account password. Apple handles its own account, payment, and purchase records under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Email, reviews, and external pages

The app provides optional links to your email app, the App Store review page, and support/legal pages. They open when you choose them. Email content and attachments that you send leave your device through your email provider and are delivered to the support address. The operator therefore receives information you voluntarily include in an inquiry. Review and web-page activity is handled by the relevant external service.

Review your message and attachments before sending. Diagnostics are not automatically attached. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information unnecessary for your inquiry.

Tokimori has no advertising, usage analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and no member registration or login.

5. Backups and removal

Tokimori does not provide its own cloud synchronization service. iOS may include app data in iCloud or computer backups according to your backup settings. Backup storage and restoration are handled by Apple or your chosen backup method.

Deleting the app removes its local app data through iOS; restoring a backup may restore data. Apple purchase records and messages already sent through email are separate. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

Changes to the published policy will be reflected on the published policy page. For questions, contact shunei.kanbara@gmail.com.
