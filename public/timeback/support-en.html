This page describes Tokimori 1.1.

Tokimori lets you choose which apps and websites stay available while the rest are restricted for a set time.

Start a lock

  1. Allow Screen Time access in Tokimori.
  2. On Home, choose the apps and websites you need during the lock. With none selected, everything is restricted except system exemptions.
  3. Choose a duration or an end time. A time that has already passed means that time tomorrow.
  4. Tap Start lock, review the end time and allowed apps/websites, then confirm.

A manual lock can last from 15 minutes to 24 hours. Within Tokimori, you cannot end an active lock early, change its allowed apps/websites, or move the end time earlier. History and Settings remain accessible. Changing settings for the next session does not change an active lock.

Tokimori itself and some system features are exempt. Check on your own device that essential calls and communication remain available. Allowing a browser does not automatically allow websites; select websites separately. Screen Time permission can be changed in iOS Settings. Tokimori cannot prevent permission revocation or guarantee a complete iPhone lock.

Restrictions end when iOS delivers the end event. This may be delayed when the phone is idle, and restrictions may temporarily behave differently after a restart. An end notification is optional and does not control whether restrictions are released.

Your previous manual time and allowed-app choices are retained for the next session.

If Screen Time permission is revoked, saved selections are cleared and schedules are disabled. After allowing access again, choose allowed apps/websites again.

Automatic locks

Create up to three schedules with weekdays and start/end times. New schedules are off. A schedule starts only after you enable it and confirm saving. Choose the weekday on which the lock should begin; an end time earlier than the start runs into the next day.

  • The configured duration must be at least 15 minutes and less than 24 hours. Enabled schedules must share a time zone and must not overlap; adjacent intervals are allowed.
  • Each schedule keeps its saved time zone when you travel. Editing its times does not automatically change that zone. Check the displayed time zone. Times absent during a daylight-saving change are skipped; repeated times use the first occurrence. An interval that fails the supported duration checks is skipped.
  • Enabling/editing a schedule during its current time interval does not start that interval retroactively. It applies from its next occurrence.
  • A manual or other active lock takes priority. A conflicting scheduled start is skipped while a lock is active. Editing/disabling a schedule does not end an already active lock.
  • Screen Time permission and either a free start or a verified, unexpired plan are required at the scheduled start. Schedules do not bypass the free allowance.
  • iOS may deliver the start event when you first use your iPhone after the scheduled time. A delayed start keeps the original end time rather than extending the session. If its eligible interval has already ended, it does not start a missed lock.
  • Open Tokimori after a subscription renews to refresh automatic-start access. If the cached plan has expired and no free starts remain, a scheduled lock will not start until purchase state is verified again.

Home displays the next planned start in your device’s current time zone. This is a planned time, not a guarantee that iOS will deliver the callback at that instant. The saved schedule’s time zone is shown in the editor.

Plans

You can try five successful session starts free. Both manual and automatic starts count toward the same allowance. Failed starts and changing settings do not consume a start. Using the free allowance does not begin a paid subscription automatically.

Monthly and annual auto-renewing plans provide the same features for continued starts. The App Store purchase confirmation shows the actual price and period. Manage/cancel a subscription in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting Tokimori does not cancel it.

After reinstalling or changing devices, try Restore purchases in Settings for your subscription. If a purchase is awaiting approval or cannot be verified, check its status before trying another purchase.

Apple handles refund requests through Report a Problem. Refund approval is not guaranteed by Tokimori.

History, privacy, and help

Session time is the interval recorded between a lock’s actual start and end, rather than a measurement of attention or time saved. History and settings are stored on your iPhone; Tokimori provides no dedicated account or cloud sync. See the Privacy Policy. Terms of Use are the Apple Standard EULA.

For questions or feedback, use Email feedback in Settings, or email shunei.kanbara@gmail.com. Include your iOS version, Tokimori version (shown in Settings), what happened, and the approximate time. You can inspect local diagnostics in Settings; the app does not automatically attach them to email. Review any details you choose to send and remove sensitive information. The App Store review link is optional.
